# Dashboard Guide

- Overview: KPI cards, time trend and class distribution.
- Neighborhood Map: local coordinate visualization.
- Pollen Drivers: component pressure analysis.
- Weather & Pollution: wind, AQI and humidity relationships.
- Seasonality: monthly patterns.
- Priority Review: configurable threshold queue.
- Scenario Lab: deterministic what-if analysis.
- Reports & Export: local Markdown and CSV outputs.
- Data Explorer: filtered records and data-quality checks.
