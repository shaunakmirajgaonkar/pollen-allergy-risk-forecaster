# Data Dictionary

`observation_id`: unique local observation ID.
`neighborhood`: local area label.
`observation_date`: observation date.
`latitude`, `longitude`: local coordinates for visualization.
`plant_species_count`: observed plant species count.
`high_pollen_species_count`: count of observed high-pollen species.
`pollen_season_factor`: 0–1 seasonal pressure factor.
`bloom_intensity_score`: 0–100 bloom intensity.
`vegetation_cover_pct`: 0–100 vegetation coverage estimate.
`temperature_c`: Celsius temperature.
`humidity_pct`: relative humidity percentage.
`wind_speed_kmh`: wind speed.
`wind_direction_deg`: wind direction.
`rainfall_mm_24h`: prior 24-hour rainfall.
`air_quality_index`: supplied AQI-like pollution signal.
`particulate_matter_ug_m3`: supplied PM signal.
`historical_pollen_level`: 0–100 historical pollen signal.
`weather_stability_score`: 0–100 weather-stability signal.
`monitoring_completeness_pct`: 0–100 completeness estimate.
